2026 USTA 8.0 Mixed Nationals — Simulation

Mixed doubles, combined 8.0: every team match is 3 mixed-doubles lines (one man + one woman per line, their NTRP ratings summing to 8.0 or less), and the tie goes to the side that wins 2 of 3. 16 sections (no Caribbean), group stage then a top-4 bracket (1v4, 2v3, final) — the same engine as the 4.0 men's board.

Match grid — predicted vs actual

One row per section. The frozen Top lineup column is that team's own D1–D3 (same every match); each match box shows the opponent's D1–D3 with a bar per line (how likely your Dk wins), plus Expected (green favored / red underdog) over Actual (fill data/results_8_0.json, re-run). UTR shown next to each name. Scroll right & down.

How D1–D3 are set: among all man+woman pairs whose combined NTRP is ≤ 8.0, each pair's strength is the average of the two players' z-scores (z = doubles UTR standardized: (UTR − league avg) ÷ league std). The strongest pair is D1, the next strongest (no player reused) is D2, then D3 — so it's the average rating, not the UTR sum. Each line bar = probability your Dk beats their Dk, from the z-score gap.

Mixed lineups

Each section's three mixed-doubles lines, strongest first. A line is one man + one woman with NTRP summing to ≤ 8.0. Line strength = average of the pair's z-scores.

Matchup matrix

Row team's probability of winning the tie (2 of 3 lines) vs each column team.
